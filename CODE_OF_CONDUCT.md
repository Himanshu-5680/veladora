# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Veladora a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What that looks like here

Be specific about a check that misfired. Leave people's private logs, names, and credentials out of issues. Día de los Muertos is a living tradition: the ofrenda in this project is a remembrance for maintainers and decisions, not a costume and not a joke about the dead.

## Enforcement

Report conduct that breaks this pledge to the maintainers through a private security advisory on GitHub, or by emailing the address on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are confidential. Maintainers will remove the content and may ban the account that posted it.

This text is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
